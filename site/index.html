modes

Three modes. Three shortcuts.

Hold a shortcut while you talk, or tap it to start and again to stop. A small bar at the bottom of the screen shows you’re being heard.

  1. dictate

    Writes what you said

    Your words, minus the “um”s, the repeats and the “no wait, I mean…”. Punctuation fixed, in any of 26 languages, or translated as you speak.

    ctrl+shift+space⌘⇧space

  2. compose

    Writes what you ask for

    Describe the email, the reply, the release notes. Speak writes it from what you said, invents nothing, and leaves a [placeholder] for what’s missing.

    ctrl+shift+alt+space⌘⇧⌥space

  3. control

    Does what you ask

    “Reply to Marc that Thursday works.” “Tidy my desktop.” The AI does it on your computer, in the apps you use, and asks first, as you choose.

    ctrl+shift+alt+enter⌘⇧⌥enter

formats

Your words, the way you need them

Clean is the default. Pick another format on the bar while you talk, or later: every dictation stays in your history with its recording, ready to redo in another format or language.

control

It does it, and asks first

Say what to do. A panel stays on top: it shows what the AI says and does, asks for your approval, and lets you pause, stop or end at any time.

What it may touch
app: only the app in front. screen: any window, no files or commands. full: everything. Speak enforces it, not the AI.
When it asks
Every step, once for the plan, when it’s risky, or never. Paying, passwords, deleting for good and sending as you always ask.
Keep working meanwhile
It clicks and types in the background, and waits for your hands to be still. Touch the mouse or keyboard, and it stops.
Undo
Files it moves, writes or trashes can be put back from the panel.

context

It spells your project right

Point Speak at a folder, a git repository or a web page. Dictating, it writes names as your project does. Composing, the AI reads the files to write the release notes or answer the issue. Read-only: nothing in your folders is changed.

privacy

Your voice stays on your computer

  • Transcribed right here

    Speech recognition runs on your own machine. Recordings are never uploaded.

  • Your own AI

    The cleanup runs through the Claude Code or Codex you already have, signed in with your account. No API keys, no new subscription.

  • No account, no servers

    Nothing to sign up for. Speak has no servers and no analytics.

  • Your history is yours

    Kept in a .speak folder in your home. Turn it off, or choose how long it’s kept.

Only text goes to your AI provider: the transcript, and the context you choose. In control mode, pictures of what it acts on go too. Without an AI provider, Speak types exactly what you said.

  1. 1

    Open it

    Speak lives in your system tray. A short setup downloads speech recognition (about 1 GB), tests your microphone and finds Claude Code or Codex.

  2. 2

    Click into any text field

    Chat, email, documents, your code editor, an AI prompt box. Nothing to integrate.

  3. 3

    Hold ctrl+shift+space⌘⇧space and talk

    Let go, and your text appears where your cursor is.

  • Windows If Windows says “Windows protected your PC” on the first launch, click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS 13.5 or later. Signed and notarized by Apple.
  • Linux Control mode needs an X11 session. On Wayland, tap the shortcut to start and stop.
  • Speed An NVIDIA graphics card or an Apple silicon Mac makes transcription almost instant.
